Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

HelloProgress (also displayed on-device as Hello Progress, package com.github.helloprogress) is designed to keep your personal event data on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally, what limited purchase and entitlement data is processed by third-party billing services, what Android permissions are used, and your rights under applicable privacy and trader transparency laws.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

HelloProgress stores your events (titles, notes, start and target dates, countdown/timer modes, reminder settings, card visual styles, colors, and fonts), home screen widget bindings, and app preferences (theme, language, layout, and Liquid Glass settings) locally on your device using Android Room database and Jetpack DataStore preference files.

We do not operate cloud servers to store or sync your personal event content. If you choose to use the local JSON Backup & Restore feature (PRO) or the Share Card image/text action, the exported backup file or generated PNG share image stays under your control and is only transferred to destinations or apps you explicitly select via the Android file picker or share sheet.

1.2 No User Accounts or Developer Cloud Sync

HelloProgress does not require account registration, sign-in, or a user profile, and does not collect your name, email address, or login credentials inside the App. Unless you disable system backup in your Android device settings, Android Auto Backup (allowBackup="true") may back up local app data to your personal Google Drive/Android backup under Google's standard Android backup controls.

1.3 Data Processed by Third-Party Services

HelloProgress uses only the billing services needed to display plans, process optional PRO purchases, and verify entitlements:

Service Purpose Data Processed
Google Play Billing Payment processing, subscription billing, trials, and refunds as merchant of record Google Play transaction records, product/base-plan identifiers, purchase tokens, and subscription renewal/cancellation state
RevenueCat Subscription and one-time lifetime entitlement management and purchase restoration Pseudonymous SDK-generated app user identifier, purchase tokens/receipts, product identifiers, and active entitlement status (does not receive your event titles, notes, dates, widgets, or local backups)

2. Permissions We Request

HelloProgress requests only the Android permissions required for its features and billing integration:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays event countdown alerts and scheduled reminders when you enable notifications.
  • Alarms & Reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Ensures time-sensitive event reminders trigger at the exact scheduled date and time.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Restores scheduled event reminders and refreshes pinned home screen widgets after your device restarts or the App updates.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Allows Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to load store offerings, complete purchases, verify PRO entitlements, and restore purchases.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): Enables in-app subscriptions and lifetime purchases through Google Play.
  • Background Scheduling (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Included by AndroidX WorkManager/Glance components to reliably complete background widget refreshes and reminder rescheduling.

3. Diagnostics, Analytics & Advertising

HelloProgress displays no third-party advertisements, integrates no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (such as Google/Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics), and does not collect or use the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).

4. Subscriptions & Purchases

HelloProgress offers optional paid upgrades (HelloProgress PRO) via monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions (with free trial offers where displayed on Google Play before checkout) and a one-time lifetime purchase. All financial payment details (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses) are handled exclusively by Google Play; the developer never receives or stores your payment card details.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Your local event data and preferences remain on your device until you delete individual events inside the App, clear app storage via Android Settings > Apps > Hello Progress > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the App.

Purchase & Support Data Deletion: Because HelloProgress does not create developer-hosted user accounts, there is no remote account profile to delete. Purchase transaction records and tax/accounting receipts are retained by Google Play and RevenueCat only for as long as needed to maintain active entitlements, restore purchases, and satisfy statutory tax or financial recordkeeping laws. For assistance or deletion inquiries regarding any support correspondence or pseudonymous RevenueCat purchase identifiers, email sumitkahar073@gmail.com. Verified requests are processed promptly and within 30 days (subject to mandatory legal retention obligations).

6. Data Security

Local databases and preferences are protected by Android application sandboxing, and billing/entitlement communications with Google Play and RevenueCat are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Because local event data resides on your device, we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock (PIN, password, or biometrics).

7. Children's Privacy

HelloProgress is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or the applicable digital age of consent in your jurisdiction, such as 13–16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact sumitkahar073@gmail.com so we can promptly delete it.

8. Third-Party Policies

Integrated billing and platform services operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play / Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including the EEA, United Kingdom, and California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, rectify, export, restrict, or request deletion of your personal data, withdraw consent for optional runtime Android permissions in device settings, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority (for example, your EEA Member State authority or the UK Information Commissioner's Office). We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
